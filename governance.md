**Agent:** P5 — multilingual support agent for an ed-tech LMS (universities and corporate academies, 14 countries, support in 6 languages, 20 agents). It answers in the customer's language from an English knowledge base and routes sensitive requests (grades, accessibility, data access) to a native-speaking agent. Trust level: L3 per language, with a per-language switch that can drop it to L2.

## Session 2 — the cost of error
- **Which cost-of-error line is a legal or reputational risk, not just money?** The **sensitive-requests** line: a wrong or automated answer on a grade, an accessibility accommodation or a data-access request is a **GDPR risk** (health data under Art. 9; a missed one-month deadline for an access request under Art. 15), a breach of the contract with the institution, and a **reputational** risk with universities, who buy on trust. This is why these requests are **never counted as resolved by the agent in `02_sizing_P05.xlsx`**: the agent detects them and routes them to a native speaker, it never answers them. Second line: a **silent quality drop in a small language** (Polish, Arabic) hidden by a good average — Simpson's paradox — is a reputational risk in that country before it is a cost.
- **Who in the company owns that risk?** The **DPO** owns the data-access and accessibility line (GDPR); the **Head of Support** owns the routing of sensitive tickets and the per-language switch; the **Product Manager** owns per-language quality and decides to open or close a language.

- **Purpose limitation (GDPR art. 5):** what we use the data for, and for how long we keep it: ticket content is used only to answer or route that ticket and to evaluate per-language quality; never to train a model, never for marketing or for profiling learners. Conversation logs (language, category, sources, model version, answer / escalate) are kept **90 days** pseudonymised, then deleted (proposal); accessibility and other sensitive content is not copied into logs beyond the routing reason. The eval-set contains no personal data and is kept for the life of the product.
- **Isolation between customers:** how the retrieval index prevents customer A's data from appearing in customer B's answer: every document carries a `tenant_id` (`global` for the public English KB); the retrieval service adds the tenant filter server-side from the authenticated session — never from the ticket text; no ticket history is indexed; the policy gate blocks an answer that cites a document from another tenant; one adversarial case per language in the eval-set from v1 (v0: `EV-PT-03`).
- **Known gaps by segment** (language, plan, region): Polish and Arabic — no native-speaker graders in the team yet, and the highest error rates in the sizing (10 % vs 4 %); Arabic — right-to-left display in the chat not tested; the 3 core languages are not confirmed; corporate academies vs universities use different words ("score" vs "grade", "programme" vs "course"); learners under 18 possible in some institutions (to check, DPO); screen-reader users not covered by the prototype; no real ticket logs at all — every share is a guess.

### Trust level per step (same scale as Session 1)
| Step | L1 suggests · L2 drafts, a person approves · L3 acts on a scope, a person samples · L4 acts alone under a threshold · or person only | Why (cost of an error) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Language detection and category | L4 above a confidence threshold; below → human queue | a wrong queue costs a delay, caught by the next person |
| 2. Sensitive-request routing | L4 in one direction only (it can send to a person, never decide to answer); the answer to a sensitive ticket = **person only** | GDPR Art. 9 / 15–17, EU AI Act Annex III, contract with the institution — legal and reputational |
| 3. Retrieval in the English KB | L3 — runs alone, a native speaker samples | a wrong article is caught by the gate (citation) or the weekly sample; €10–25 per wrong answer |
| 4. Answer in the customer's language | L3 for each core language that passes its eval · L2 for Portuguese, Polish, Arabic until they pass | errors cost more in small languages (PL / AR 10 % error, €25 per error) and a good average can hide them |
| 5. Policy gate and send | gate L4 (block → human) · send: L3 languages by the agent with an "AI answer" label and "talk to a person"; L2 languages by the native speaker | a promise the company cannot keep (grade, deadline, deletion) |
| 6. Logging | L4 | without logs per language, the Simpson's-paradox risk cannot be seen |
