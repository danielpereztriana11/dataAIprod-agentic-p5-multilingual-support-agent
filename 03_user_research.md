## Interview guide (adapt it — keep it about the past)
1. Tell me about the last time you got stuck on your LMS (or answered someone who was stuck). What happened, step by step?
2. What was the hardest part? How long did it take before it was solved?
3. What did you try to solve it — help-centre article, a translator, a classmate, a ticket? What did that cost you?
4. When it went wrong (no answer, wrong answer, answer in the wrong language), what did you do? Who did you ask?
5. If you could change one thing in that moment, what would it be?
*For #2 (support side):* the last ticket you answered in a language that was not the article's language — how did you find the article, how did you translate it, how did you know a request had to go to someone else (grades, accessibility, personal data)?
*Avoid:* “Would you use an AI that…?” · “Do you like this idea?” · “How much would you pay?”
